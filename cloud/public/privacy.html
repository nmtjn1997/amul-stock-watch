Back in Stock

Privacy

Back in Stock is a free, unofficial tool that tells you when an Amul product is back in stock at your pincode. It is not affiliated with Amul.

What we store

Your username and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), the products and pincodes you watch, and the places you asked us to send alerts: browser push addresses, a Telegram chat id, an ntfy topic, a Slack or Discord webhook, and an email address you confirmed with a code. We also keep a short log of sign-ins and alerts (kept 30 days) to stop abuse.

How it is used

Only to send you the alerts you set up. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads. Email alerts are sent from backinstock.pings@gmail.com, which can send mail but has no access to read anyone's mailbox.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Delete my account removes your account and everything linked to it straight away.
